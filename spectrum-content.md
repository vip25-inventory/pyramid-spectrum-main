## About Us

**Spectrum by Pyramid Academy** (established in 2006) is a premier training institute in Vellore and Katpadi, dedicated to academic excellence and creative development. With over **12+ years of experience** and having trained more than **10,000+ students**, we specialize in offering programs that foster communication, academic achievement, and self-confidence.

**Vision:**  
To empower individuals through skill-based education, focusing on fluency in English and academic performance in core subjects like Maths, Physics, Chemistry, and Biology.

**Our Locations:**
- **Vellore:** Officer’s Line, City Centre, Opp. Voorhees School  
- **Katpadi:** Jagannathan Nagar, Gandhi Nagar, Near Auxilium College  

**Why Choose Us?**
- Effective and customized training methods  
- Expert and efficient trainers  
- Hands-on learning: Live classes, assessments, discussions  
- Friendly and supportive environment  
- Flexible batch timings
